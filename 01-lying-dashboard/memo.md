# Memo: Q3 conversion rate audit

**Result.** The dashboard's "CVR up to 4.43%" is a calculation error: it averages the three channel rates as if each had the same traffic. The true CVR fell from 4.36% (Q2) to 2.67% (Q3): −1.69 pp, −39% relative.

**Why.** Every channel improved (Search +0.5 pp, Social +0.3 pp, Email +0.5 pp), but Paid Social, the weakest channel at 1.3%, grew from 18% to 69% of sessions. That mix shift cost roughly 280 bookings in one quarter (≈628 at Q2's mix vs 347 actual).

**Recommendation.** Don't move more budget into Paid Social on the basis of CVR. Shift back toward Paid Search, the highest-converting channel we can scale (Email converts best but is capped by list size). Fix the dashboard: overall CVR = total conversions ÷ total sessions.

**What would change this.** CVR isn't profit. If cost and revenue per channel show Paid Social is much cheaper per booking (lower CPA, higher ROAS), its role may justify the spend, so we need spend and revenue by channel before a final budget call.

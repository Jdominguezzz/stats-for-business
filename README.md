# Statistics for business decisions

Short, end-to-end projects where statistics changes a real business decision, in **marketing**, **finance** (banking, investments) and **sports** (football, Formula 1).
Each project: a business question → the method (by hand first, then Python) → the result with its limits → a one-page decision memo.

| # | Project | Niche | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [The lying campaign dashboard](01-lying-dashboard/) | Marketing · paid media | Audited a dashboard that reported conversion rate up 4.0% → 4.4% when it had actually fallen 4.4% → 2.7%: an average of channel rates hid a traffic shift into the weakest channel (Simpson's paradox), costing ≈280 bookings in one quarter. |

**Skills:** weighted averages · percentage points vs relative change · Simpson's paradox · Python (csv, pathlib, matplotlib) · decision memos
